# theNineTailed
A knuckle-headed maverick - once an outcast, now a hero.

Fight your way up the Spire with Clones, the help of Kurama's Tails, and the awe-inspiring might of Talk no Jutsu.

Major themes:
- Clones
- Tails
- Powers
- Discard
- Rasen
- Understanding
- Calm and Divinity
Character art from Naruto Shippuden: Ultimate Ninja Blazing, card art from the anime, and relic/Orb art drawn from scratch or reworked from base-game by me.
